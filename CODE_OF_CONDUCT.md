# Code of Conduct

**Organisation:** Quectosoft Technologies LLP
**Maintained by:** Subrit Dikshit · subrit@quectosofttech.com · subrit@gmail.com

---

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making this project a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free space for everyone — students, researchers, professionals, and contributors of all backgrounds, nationalities, disciplines, and experience levels.

## Our Standards

✅ Use welcoming and inclusive language
✅ Respect differing viewpoints and experiences
✅ Accept constructive criticism gracefully and professionally
✅ Focus on what is best for the community
✅ Show empathy towards other community members

❌ Harassment, trolling, or personal attacks of any kind
❌ Publishing others' private information without explicit consent
❌ Discriminatory language or behaviour based on any personal characteristic
❌ Unsolicited commercial promotion or spam
❌ Sustained disruptive behaviour in discussions or reviews

## Enforcement

Violations may be reported to: **subrit@quectosofttech.com**
All reports are reviewed confidentially. Reporters will not face retaliation.

Consequences range from a warning to permanent ban, depending on severity and history.

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
